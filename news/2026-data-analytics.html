Turning Web Analytics Into Decisions at UAB

At UAB I manage web platforms for four departments, which means more than keeping sites online. I set up Google Tag Manager and Looker Studio to track how people use them: what they search for, where they drop off, and which pages turn into service requests.

That data now decides what gets worked on: which pages get rebuilt, which flows get simplified, and where the friction actually is rather than where we assumed it was.
