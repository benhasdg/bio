The Stone Paver Patio Project

In the summer of 2020 I built a multi-level stone paver patio from scratch—no contractor, no kit, just some research, a rented plate compactor, and a lot of weekends.

The finished patio—now home to an increasingly large Monstera.

The site started as a blank slate—just grass and a slope headed straight for the house. First step was clearing the area and grading it so water would drain away from the foundation instead of toward it.

The starting point: Site clearing and initial grading.

Next came the retaining wall, built from heavy masonry block to hold up the elevated section of the patio. I backfilled it with crushed stone and built the base up in layers, tamping each one down so it wouldn't settle later.

This part ran almost entirely on hand tools—a level, a plate compactor, a rubber mallet, and a lot of patience. Getting the pattern right meant checking the level constantly; one paver set even slightly high throws off everything that comes after it.

Tools for the job—mostly leveling and tamping gear.

It took most of a summer, but the patio's held up since—still level, still draining the right way, without a crack in it.
